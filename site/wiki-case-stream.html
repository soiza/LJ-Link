Use caseLivestream and privacy mode

No titles on any screen, nothing else changes.

Whenlivestream, screen share, photos or video of the booth, unreleased tracks
StatusStable

What it hides

LJ Link then sends no titles, artists, albums, genres, labels, remixers, comments and covers at all, also not to tablets or other computers. Waveforms, times, tempo and cues stay visible.

Switch it on

  1. Click the program icon (menu bar on a Mac, next to the clock on Windows).

  2. Tick Privacy mode. The interface shows PRIVACY.

  3. To start in this mode every time: start LJ Link with --privacy.

iInfo

On purpose it can only be switched from the program icon, not from the browser. Nobody with a tablet can switch it off during the stream.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
