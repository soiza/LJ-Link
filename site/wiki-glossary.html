HelpGlossary

Every technical word of this wiki in one sentence.

Terms

TermMeaning
Pro DJ LinkPioneer’s network for CDJs, XDJs and DJM mixers. Players share tracks, beats and status with each other.
StageLinQDenon’s network interface for Engine DJ devices, used by Engine DJ, SoundSwitch and LJ Link.
Ableton LinkA protocol that keeps the tempo of programs in step over the network.
OSCOpen Sound Control: short messages with an address and a value over UDP. grandMA3, Daslight, Resolume and many programs take it.
OS2LOpen Sound to Light: beats and tempo from DJ software to light software over TCP. Used by VirtualDJ and the Wolfmix.
TelnetA text connection to the command line of a device. The grandMA2 takes commands this way.
Speed masterA master on the light desk that sets the speed of the effects and chasers assigned to it.
SEND BPMThe tab in LJ Link that chooses which deck gives the tempo to the desk.
Master (DJ)The player the others sync to. Its BPM box is orange in LJ Link.
ON AIRThe mixer channel is up and sound is at the master output. Red LIVE frame in LJ Link.
Beat gridThe beats of a track, analysed by rekordbox, Engine DJ or Serato. Red ticks mark bar starts.
Hot cue, memory cueMarked points in the track: hot cues A–H as flags, memory cues as red triangles.
SwitchConnects network cables to one network.
Router, DHCPA router hands out addresses (DHCP) and often has Wi-Fi.
Link-local, 169.254The address a device gives itself when there is no router.
Subnet maskSays which part of the address is the network. With 255.255.255.0 the first three numbers must be equal.
127.0.0.1The computer itself. For a program on the same computer.
onPCThe light desk software of MA Lighting on a normal computer.
Art-Net, sACN, MA-NetNetworks for lighting data (DMX over Ethernet) and MA desks. Usually kept apart from the DJ network.
Privacy modeLJ Link sends no titles, artists or covers. Switched only from the program icon.
