DJ sourcesDenon StageLinQ

Engine DJ devices with real fader positions.

Devices

DeviceStatusNotes
Prime 4+ (firmware 5.0.4)Stabletitle, artist, tempo, play state, master, position, channel faders, waveform, beat grid, cues; loops not verified
SC6000, SC6000M, Prime 4, Prime Go, Prime 2Expectedsame protocol, not tested yet

Connecting

Denon devices speak StageLinQ, the same interface Engine DJ and SoundSwitch use. The device must be in the same network as the computer, by cable or Wi-Fi.

  1. Connect the device by cable to the switch or router (recommended), or join both to the same Wi-Fi.

  2. Close Engine DJ, SoundSwitch and other StageLinQ programs on the computer that runs LJ Link.

  3. Search automatically. The device appears with all its decks; the first time LJ Link loads its library, which can take a moment.

What you get

  • Real fader positions in the blue bar (Pioneer cannot do that). EQ is not sent.
  • Exact position of every deck.
  • Waveform with beat grid, bar starts, hot cues and loops from the Engine library on the device’s drive or card, loaded once. It has overview resolution; the detail view is smoothed from it.
  • Covers only if the library stores them.

Problems

The device is not found
  • Same network? Check the addresses (basics).
  • Engine DJ or SoundSwitch open on this computer? Close them, they use the same port.
  • Guest Wi-Fi or a mesh repeater? Use cable.
It keeps reconnecting

Update to LJ Link 1.3.1 or newer. After a broken connection the device needs a few seconds before it accepts a new one; that is normal.

No waveform for a track

The track must be analysed in Engine DJ or on the device. Tracks from streaming services have no stored waveform.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
