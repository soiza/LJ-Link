DJ sourcesPioneer Pro DJ Link

CDJs, XDJs and DJM mixers over the network.

Devices

DeviceStatusNotes
CDJ-3000Stablestatus, metadata, waveforms, cues, loops, exact position
CDJ-2000NXS2Stableall of it, position from beats and beat grid; no active loop reported
DJM-2000NXSStableas mixer: ON AIR per channel
CDJ-2000NXS, CDJ-900NXS, XDJ-1000MK2, XDJ-XZ, XDJ-AZExpectedsame protocol, not tested yet
DJM-900NXS2, DJM-A9, DJM-V10Expectedas mixer, not tested yet

What you need

  • Players and mixer by cable on one switch, router or the DJM’s LINK sockets. See setups compared.
  • Tracks on a USB stick or SD card exported with rekordbox (export mode, tracks analysed).
  • Every player its own player number: on the player *Utility → Player No.*
  • No rekordbox on the computer that runs LJ Link.

Step by step

  1. Connect all players, the mixer and the computer by cable. Switch Wi-Fi off on the computer.

  2. Check the player numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, each only once. The mixer channel of each player should match its number, otherwise ON AIR shows the wrong deck.

  3. Start LJ Link → Search automatically. Within a few seconds every player appears, the mixer shows up in the Mixer tab.

  4. Load a track. Title, cover and waveforms arrive; with several players this can take a few seconds.

iInfo

LJ Link joins the network as a virtual player with its own number, like other Pro DJ Link programs do. It only listens and sends no sync, master or tempo commands.

Limits of Pro DJ Link

  • Faders and EQ: no DJM sends them. The blue bar only shows on air or off.
  • ON AIR also needs sound at the master output and matching channel numbers.
  • Position on NXS2 and older: they report only between beats. LJ Link runs them at their pitch, so the waveform moves smoothly; after a cue or loop there can be small jumps. Scratching is shown only roughly.
  • Streaming tracks (Beatport, SoundCloud, TIDAL …), CDs and never analysed files have no waveform data.
  • CDJ-3000 with new firmware: if a stick from an older rekordbox shows no waveform, export it again with rekordbox 6 or 7.

Problems

A player appears twice
  • Wi-Fi and cable in the same network: switch Wi-Fi off.
  • Two players with the same number, or LJ Link started twice.
  • Virtual adapters (VPN, Parallels, VMware, Docker): switch them off.
“No track loaded” or no waveform
  • Update to LJ Link 1.4.1 or newer.
  • Track from a rekordbox export? Streaming and CD have no data.
  • rekordbox on this computer? Quit it.
Yellow note “Beat port 50001 is in use”

rekordbox, Beat Link Trigger, SoundSwitch or a second LJ Link listens for beats. Close it and search again. Until then positions are estimated.
