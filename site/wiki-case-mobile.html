Use caseMobile with Denon and Wolfmix

One laptop, one controller, one Wolfmix.

βBeta

The Wolfmix part is beta: tried on a Wolfmix W1, where Ableton Link worked better than OS2L. Keep the Wolfmix microphone or tap as a fallback.

GearDenon Prime (or CDJ), one laptop, Wolfmix on USB
Networksmall travel router, or a cable from the Prime to the laptop
StatusDenon stable Wolfmix beta

network cableWi-FiUSBsame computer

Steps

  1. Router on, then the Prime and the laptop by cable (Wi-Fi also works for the Prime).

  2. LJ Link → Search automatically. The Prime’s decks appear with faders and waveforms.

  3. Wolfmix on USB, WTOOLS on, OS2L on.

  4. LJ Link: LINK → Wolfmix → 127.0.0.1, 5000 → Save → Send test.

  5. Wolfmix: push the first encoder in the FX rack. Effects follow the beats.

  6. Optional: the phone in the router’s Wi-Fi shows the waveforms (tablet).

Without a router

A cable from the Prime straight to the laptop also works (direct cable), with Wi-Fi off on the laptop. Then there is no phone view.

✓Checklist before the doors open

  • Test beats arrive in WTOOLS.
  • Fallback (mic or tap) ready on the Wolfmix.
  • Report how it went: Discord.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
