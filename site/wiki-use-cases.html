Quick decision

QuestionAnswer
Direct cable or router?Router or switch, always when you can. Direct cable only for one player. Compared
Wi-Fi or cable?Cable for Pioneer and the computer. Wi-Fi is fine for tablets and Denon.
Where does LJ Link run?On a computer in the DJ network. With Serato: on the DJ’s laptop.
Which desk works best?grandMA2, onPC and Daslight are tested. grandMA3 and Wolfmix are beta.
Several floors?One LJ Link computer per floor, and under Settings → Devices tick only the devices of that floor.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
