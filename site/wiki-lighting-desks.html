How the link works

LJ Link reads the tempo of the deck chosen under SEND BPM and sends it to the desk. The desk puts it on a speed master; every effect and chaser assigned to that master runs in time.

  • Rounded to 0.1 BPM. Since 1.7.4 the sent tempo only changes when the smoothed tempo of the player moves more than 0.15 BPM away from it, so a player at a rounding edge (137.95) no longer makes it jump. A jump of 0.25 BPM or more (pitch fader, new track) goes through in under a second. A fixed value, the tempo of an Ableton Link session as source and Serato go out exactly, without this dead band. Since 1.7.5 the BPM on the deck shows the real value of the player (two decimals, not yet tested on hardware); the sent tempo is the tooltip on the BPM field. Not yet tested on hardware; see Tempo on the light desk jumps.
  • Repeated when it stays the same: OSC every 2 seconds, grandMA2 every 15 seconds (so the command line does not fill up).
  • OS2L (Wolfmix) gets every single beat, so the desk also knows where the bar starts.
  • LJ Link only sets the tempo. It starts no cues and changes nothing in the show.
  • The demo only sends when Send demo data is ticked in the light desk settings (since 1.7.2, off at every start).

✕Important

A speed master only moves effects and chasers that are assigned to it. If the master changes but nothing happens on stage, the effect is almost always on another master.

SEND BPM: which deck gives the tempo

ChoiceBehaviourGood for
Autothe deck that is master and/or live; with two systems the master that is also live and playingnormal nights, one system
One deckalways this deck, even if another one is live; without a track nothing is sentAbleton Link as source, tempo jumping between two masters

The deck that sends wears a green SEND BPM in every view, tablets included. The choice stays after a restart and can only be changed on the computer itself.

Set up in LJ Link

  1. Menu (☰) → Light desk link (send tempo), or the tab LINK.

  2. Choose the desk. The fields below change to what this desk needs, with a hint.

  3. Address and port, for grandMA2 also user and password. onPC on the same computer: 127.0.0.1.

  4. Save, then Send test (120 BPM). Below it *Last sent* shows the value or an error.

Status messages

MessageMeaning
“Sending the tempo is switched off”no desk chosen
“Waiting for a deck with a tempo …”no deck plays or has a track
“The chosen deck has no tempo right now”fixed deck under SEND BPM is empty
“Demo mode: nothing is sent to the desk”demo runs
“Connection refused”desk not reachable on this port (grandMA2: Telnet off)
“No OS2L receiver at …”WTOOLS not running or OS2L off
