Use caseClub with CDJs and grandMA2

The classic club setup, from the booth to the speed master.

Gear2–4 CDJ/XDJ, DJM, grandMA2 console or onPC at FOH
Networkone switch or router in the booth, one long cable to FOH
Resultwaveforms at FOH, tempo on Speed 16 (BPM)
StatusStable

network cableWi-FiUSBsame computer

What you need

  • A switch or router in the booth with a free port, or a free LINK socket on the DJM.
  • A network cable to FOH. Up to 100 m is fine for Ethernet; use a good Cat5e/Cat6 cable.
  • A laptop with LJ Link (and a USB network adapter if it has no socket).
  • Access to the grandMA2 show: Telnet on, a user with rights.

Steps

  1. Booth: check that all players and the DJM are on the switch and have different player numbers.

  2. Cable to FOH: plug the long cable into the switch and the laptop. Switch Wi-Fi off on the laptop.

  3. Search: start LJ Link, Search automatically. All players and the mixer appear.

  4. Desk in the network: plug the grandMA2 into the same switch, or use its own network (two networks). Check that addresses fit (basics).

  5. Desk: Setup → Console → Global Settings → Telnet Login Enabled. Effects that should follow the tempo on Speed 16 (BPM).

  6. LJ Link: LINK → grandMA2 → Search for the desk → click it → user/password → Save → Send test. The master jumps to 120.

  7. SEND BPM on Auto: the tempo follows the master deck that is on air.

✓Checklist before the doors open

  • Every player shows a track and a waveform.
  • The red LIVE frame follows the channel that is up.
  • Green SEND BPM sits on the right deck, the master on the desk follows a tempo change.
  • Laptop on mains power, sleep mode off.

During the night

  • A DJ changeover with new players: they are added by themselves; old ones disappear after a few seconds.
  • Tempo jumps between two decks (both master)? Fix SEND BPM to one deck for this moment.
  • Want your effects to follow only in parts of the set? Leave the link on and switch the effect to another speed master on the desk.
