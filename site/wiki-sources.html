✓Tip

LJ Link can also send to Ableton Link and lead the session with the tempo, beats and bars of a Pioneer or Denon deck, tested with Daslight: Ableton Link → Send.

All sources can run at the same time: Search automatically looks for all of them, and the decks appear side by side.
