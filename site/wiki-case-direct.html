Use caseDirect cable

One cable, no switch. Quick, with one catch.

Gearone player or controller, one network cable, a computer
Whenquick rehearsal, a single controller (XDJ-RX, Prime), an emergency
StatusWorks, with a known bug

What works

  • One device directly: a CDJ, an all-in-one controller or a Denon Prime.
  • Into a DJM LINK socket instead: all players on the mixer are seen. That is the better direct cable.
  • No router: both sides give themselves 169.254.x.x addresses. Wait up to a minute after plugging in.

Steps

  1. Switch Wi-Fi off on the computer. This is the most important step.

  2. Plug the cable into the player (LINK socket) and the computer (or USB adapter).

  3. Wait 30–60 seconds, then Search automatically.

  4. Nothing? Set up manually, enter the player’s address (Utility → Link Info) and Check connection.

✕Important

Known bug: with a direct cable and Wi-Fi on, players sometimes show up twice or not at all. Quit LJ Link (icon → Quit) and start again; switch Wi-Fi off; or put a small switch between. If you see it, please send a report while it is on screen.

Better: a small switch

A 5-port switch costs little, needs no setup and removes most problems of the direct cable. Keep one in the bag.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
