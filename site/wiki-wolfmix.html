Light desks · betaWolfmix

Ableton Link (recommended) or every beat over OS2L.

βStatus

Tried on a Wolfmix W1 with two XDJ-1000MK2. Ableton Link worked better there than OS2L and is the recommended way. Over OS2L the tempo jittered by a tenth and the Wolfmix sometimes restarted its beat (double flashes, gaps). 1.7.3 takes the beats straight from the players’ beat packets, and 1.7.4 keeps the tempo steady when a player stands at a rounding edge such as 137.95 BPM. Neither fix has been tried on the Wolfmix yet.

Ableton Link (recommended)

Use Ableton Link whenever you can. The Wolfmix manual (chapter 8, pages 19–20) calls Ableton Link very accurate, because the Wolfmix takes the BPM calculated by the DJ software, here LJ Link. In our test it also ran more smoothly than OS2L.

  1. In LJ Link: Menu (☰) → Light desk link → Wolfmix (Ableton Link) – recommended. It switches on Ableton Link further down in the menu if it is off.

  2. In WTOOLS choose Ableton Link as the source of the tempo. WTOOLS and LJ Link must be in the same network or on the same computer.

  3. On the Wolfmix push the first encoder in the FX rack, so the effects follow the BPM (manual, chapter 8).

  4. Under SEND BPM choose the deck (or Auto). The BPM on the Wolfmix follows it, with beat and bar.

OS2L

✎Note

OS2L is the alternative when Ableton Link is not possible. In our test the Wolfmix sometimes restarted its beat when the tempo changed over OS2L. Why is not documented by the maker; LJ Link now only sends a new tempo on a real change, but this is not tried on the Wolfmix yet. If it happens to you, switch to Ableton Link.

How it works

The Wolfmix has no network, only USB and DMX. The tempo comes through the maker’s app WTOOLS on the computer the Wolfmix is plugged into. LJ Link talks to WTOOLS over OS2L (Open Sound to Light, the way VirtualDJ does it) and sends every beat with its number, not only the tempo. Every fourth beat is the one of the bar, matching the beat grid of the player.

Step by step

  1. Plug the Wolfmix into the computer by USB and start WTOOLS. Switch on OS2L there.

  2. In LJ Link: Light desk link → Wolfmix (OS2L, WTOOLS) – Beta.

  3. Address 127.0.0.1 if WTOOLS runs on the same computer, otherwise the address of the WTOOLS computer. Port 5000 as in the Wolfmix manual. A post in the VirtualDJ forum uses port 9996 instead, so if nothing arrives, check the OS2L port in WTOOLS and enter that one. Save.

  4. Send test: eight beats at 120 BPM.

  5. On the Wolfmix push the first encoder in the FX rack, so the effects follow the BPM.

What is sent

  • A beat message for every beat of the deck under SEND BPM, with beat number and tempo. With Pioneer players the beat comes straight from their beat packets, so it is exactly on the beat even without rekordbox data.
  • The tempo is rounded to 0.1 BPM and only changes when the smoothed tempo of the player moves more than 0.15 BPM away from the sent value. A player standing at a rounding edge (137.95, swinging between 137.9 and 138.0) no longer makes the tempo jump; a change of 0.25 BPM or more goes through in under a second, smaller deviations of up to 0.15 BPM are deliberately not sent. The BPM on the deck shows the real player value since 1.7.5 (not tested on hardware yet); the sent tempo is its tooltip. Not yet tried on the Wolfmix. See Tempo on the light desk jumps.
  • change after start, a jump (cue, loop), a track or deck change or a tempo step of at least 0.5 BPM, so the desk finds the phase again.
  • A standing deck sends no beats. Serato and Ableton Link have no position: there the beats run freely at the tempo.
  • If WTOOLS is not running, LJ Link shows it in the status and tries again every 5 seconds.

What is not known yet

  • Whether the fixes of 1.7.3 and 1.7.4 remove the double flashes and beat restarts on the Wolfmix over OS2L.
  • Which OS2L port WTOOLS uses by default (5000 in the manual, 9996 in a forum post).
  • Whether WTOOLS accepts connections from another computer.
  • How much delay USB adds between WTOOLS and the Wolfmix.
