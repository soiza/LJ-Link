Use caseSeveral DJs, mixed gear

Pioneer and Denon in one view, through every changeover.

Gearhouse CDJs and DJM, plus a guest DJ with a Denon Prime 4+ or an XDJ-AZ
Networkeverything on one switch or router
Resultall decks side by side, tempo from whoever plays
StatusStable

How LJ Link handles it

  • One Search automatically finds Pioneer and Denon at the same time and keeps looking while it runs.
  • A device that comes later is added; one that leaves disappears after a few seconds.
  • The Mixer tab shows one block per mixer: the DJM with ON AIR, the Denon with real faders.
  • SEND BPM Auto with two systems: the master that is also live and playing wins.

Changeover step by step

  1. Before the night: a free port on the switch with a cable ready for the guest.

  2. Guest plugs in the Prime 4+. Within seconds its decks appear next to the CDJs.

  3. During the overlap: SEND BPM Auto follows the system that is live. If it jumps, set SEND BPM to the deck that plays.

  4. After the change: put SEND BPM back to Auto. The old devices disappear when they are switched off.

!Warning

A guest controller like the XDJ-AZ also uses Pro DJ Link: it needs player numbers that the house CDJs do not use.

Many devices or floors

Under Settings → Devices tick devices on or off. With two floors in one network, each LJ Link computer shows only its floor.

✓Checklist before the doors open

  • Guest device seen before the change.
  • Player numbers unique.
  • SEND BPM back on Auto.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
