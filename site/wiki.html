What LJ Link does

LJ Link shows the lighting jockey what the DJ is playing: every deck with waveform, cues, beat grid, time and tempo, live in the browser. On request it also sends the tempo to the light desk, so chasers and effects run in time with the music.

  • Passive: LJ Link only listens. It never sends sync, tempo or fader commands to the DJ gear.
  • Local: no internet, no cloud, no account. Only the update check uses the internet.
  • Free and open source for macOS and Windows.

What the status means

Every device and program in this wiki carries one of these labels. We only call something stable when it ran on the real device.

StatusMeaning
StableTested on the real device.
BetaBuilt from the maker’s manual or protocol and tested against a simulation, but not yet confirmed on the real device.
AlphaNew and never tried on the real device. It may not work at all. Do not rely on it at an important event.
UnconfirmedLJ Link sends, but it is not known whether the program can use it.
GenericA generic output that you connect yourself in the target program.
PlannedPlanned, not built yet.
Not possibleThe device cannot receive this over a network.

💬Something missing or wrong?

Ask in our Discord or report a problem. Reports from real gear help most, also when something does not work.
